## What the site is
- A free QR code generator. Free because it is ad-supported (Google AdSense, which uses cookies). No subscription, no account, no signup, no email required.
- QR types: URL, WiFi, vCard, Email, Text, PDF. Nothing else (no SMS, no social-profile type, no app-store type, no payment type).
- Downloads: PNG and SVG only.
- Customization in the generator: colors, frames, shapes, and uploading a logo.
- Dynamic codes that never expire: the code points to a short redirect link, so the destination can be changed later without reprinting.
- Dashboard (/dashboard): shows scans, devices, countries and hours for your codes. There is no login: the codes and the session are saved in the browser you used, so they are tied to that browser/device.
- PDF type: you can upload a PDF (PDF only, up to 20 MB) or paste a link. Uploaded PDFs are stored on the site's server and served from a trulyfreeqr.link URL. Do not claim PDF uploads stay on the user's device.

## Things the site does NOT offer (never claim them)
- Self-serve custom domains or a public API (the site only says enterprise users can contact support about them), white-label, team accounts, logins, bulk/CSV generation, built-in A/B split testing (users can only change a code's destination by hand), retargeting pixels, password-protected codes, expiring codes, print services.

## Claims to reject
- Statistics, percentages or case-study results without a named, checkable source.
- Anything specific about competitors' pricing, limits or features.
- "Never expire" applies to our dynamic codes; do not generalize it to other services.
